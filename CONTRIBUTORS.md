# Contributors

Each person below has agreed to [Konzilo's contributor agreement](CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT.md), in the version their line names, by adding the line in a commit of their own. [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) says when that is needed.

A line gives your name, your user name on GitHub, the agreement's version, and whom you contribute for: yourself, or the employer or other who has rights in what you write. Add yours at the end of the list, like this:

```
- Ada Example, ada-example, version 1, for myself
- Ben Example, ben-example, version 1, for Example AB
```
