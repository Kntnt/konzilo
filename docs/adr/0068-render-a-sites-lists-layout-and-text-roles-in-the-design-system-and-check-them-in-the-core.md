# ADR 0068: Render a site's lists, layout and text roles in the design system, and check them in the core

**Date:** 2026-11-10, amended 2026-11-11, 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-25 · **Status:** Accepted, settled by Thomas on 2026-11-10: these components render in the design system, a site declares them, and the core checks them. The markup is agreed with the design system's session on 2026-11-10, and is its own, in its `docs/spec/directives.md`, with classes and attributes of its own. That a component is given its body as a tree as well, with the block marks on it, is settled by Thomas (issue #34, 2026-11-11); that the tree is hast, in the prop `content` of every component with a body, is a technical choice he may veto. That a page in the live preview looks as it does published, a component within another included, its mark on its own root rather than in a wrapper inside the other, is settled by Thomas (issue #35, 2026-11-11); technical choices he may veto (2026-11-12): a component is given its block's mark as the prop `data-konzilo-block`, to place on its root; where it does not, the core puts the mark on the element the component rendered at its top, or on each of those it rendered there side by side, a script or a style aside; and where no element can hold it, a wrapper with no box of its own holds it at the top of a body, and nothing within another component's body. That a container is given each block at the top of its body through a slot of its own, drawn by the core as its default slot draws it, rather than a component of the core's handed to it as a prop, is a technical choice he may veto (issue #55, 2026-11-25), and so is the slot's name, `block-<index>` by the block's index in `content.children`, with no slot for text of whitespace alone or a comment there, and none for a leaf's label. That a template is given its post's body as a tree too, as a component's `content` is, is asked by issue #83; technical choices he may veto (2026-11-25): the helper's name, `bodyTree()` of `@kntnt/konzilo/astro`; that from a post's entry it gives the tree `content` is, of the whole body, a component and a post list as their plain rendering, rendered through Astro's container as a feed renders a body; and that it takes HTML as well, giving the tree of what a template rendered through `Body`, each component as the site's component drew it and each post list by its presentation.

## Decision

- **The design system renders them.** Its package holds the Astro components for the containers that lay a page out, hero, section and aside, with the rules by which a section's layout follows from what it holds; the list, with its kinds and markers, and the five components that show a list in another form, cards, accordion, tabs, table of contents and choice, each a directive of its own beside the list; and the text roles, an eyebrow over a heading, small print, and the breadcrumbs. A site registers each component through `konzilo({ directives })`, by the package's name, as it does its presentations (ADR 0054).
- **A site takes their declarations from the package** (ADR 0060), each with its label, its form, its body and its attributes, by `componentsFrom`, with labels of its own in each language, and a type's `elements` names the ones it takes. The core has no names of its own for them, so a site without the design system may declare others as its own components, or none.
- **The core checks what they hold,** their attributes by the rule data's patterns, and the rest by the package's check, which the site's `componentChecks` names, in the intake, before anything is published.
- **The core keeps their plain rendering,** a `div` with the directive's name and attributes as `data-` attributes, holding its body, so that a site without the design system builds, and its own styles can draw them.
- **What the core writes stays the core's** (ADR 0054): consent, embeds, forms, post lists, search, the pager and the breadcrumbs' levels. The design system styles them by the classes and custom properties in `docs/site-design.md`, and renders its own components around them.
- **A component is given its body as a tree as well,** beside its default slot: a hast `root` in the prop `content`, for every component with a body, read by Sätteri from the body's HTML as the core renders it, with the live preview's marks on its blocks as `dataKonziloBlock`, and a component within as its plain rendering, holding its own body. A design lays a component out by the tree, such as a list whose items begin at its headings, a deck's length, whether every heading is a question, or a lone `tel:` or `mailto:` link in a paragraph, and a simple component places its slot. A component drawn from the tree keeps the marks by drawing each element with its properties.
- **A container is given each block of its body through a slot of its own** as well, beside its default slot and its tree: the node at index `i` of `content.children` through the slot `block-<i>`, drawn by the core exactly as the default slot draws it, a component within by the site's component, a post list by its presentation, a form, an image with its lightbox, and in the live preview the block's mark. A node there that is no block, text of whitespace alone or a comment, takes its index and has no slot, and a leaf, whose label holds no blocks, has none. A component asks for a block's slot by its name, by ``<slot name={`block-${index}`} />`` or `Astro.slots.render()`, in any order and as often as it likes, and a slot it never asks for is never rendered. Each slot is drawn from the parts the body was read into once, the same parts the default slot is drawn from, so a post list in it is evaluated once, in its place among the body's lists, whichever slots the component renders; and each dialog the lightbox writes takes its number as it is drawn, so a block drawn twice has two.
- **A template is given its post's body as a tree as well,** by `bodyTree(entry)` of `@kntnt/konzilo/astro`: the tree `content` is, of the whole body, read from the HTML Astro's `render()` gives the post and `Body` draws its components from, so a heading holds its ID, an image is as the page draws it but for the lightbox, a component and a post list stand as their plain rendering, holding their bodies, and in the live preview each block holds its mark, as on the page; a component's `content` is its part of that tree. Given HTML, `bodyTree(html)` gives that HTML's tree, read as `content` is, so that a template's own component around `Body` reads the page's rendering of the body, `bodyTree(await Astro.slots.render('default'))`, each component as the site's component drew it, with the anchor it writes, each post list by its presentation, and in the live preview a component's mark on its root. A template builds a table of contents from either, of its crossheads and the components that carry an anchor. One that draws a block from the tree with its properties keeps the block's mark, so that a click on it leads to the block, as on a component that draws its tree; a table of contents, which points at a block rather than draws it, leaves the mark off, since the page shows a block a field asks for at the first element that holds the mark.
- **In the live preview, a component's block is marked on its own root.** A component the site renders is given its block's mark as the prop `data-konzilo-block`, which it places on its root, as an attribute. Where it places it on no element, the core puts it on the element the component rendered at its top, or on each of those it rendered there side by side, a script, a style and the like aside. Where none can hold it, the component having rendered text at its top, no element, or its blocks with nothing around them, a wrapper with no box of its own holds the mark at the top of a body; within another component's body nothing does, the blocks the component holds leading to it, and a click on the rest to the component around it. A component on the public site is given no mark, and nothing is added to what it renders.
- **The contracts between the two are in `docs/site-design.md`:** the breadcrumbs' last level, given with `hidden: true` where the breadcrumbs end above the page; the marks of each block in the live preview, which a component the design renders keeps on the blocks it is given, and places on its own root; and the opening of the `<details>` a block stands in, with the event `konzilo-preview-show` for a design that opens anything else. The design system draws each tab and each answer as a `<details>` of an exclusive group, so it needs no script for this.

## Why

- How a list of cards, an accordion or a section's layout looks is design, decided once for every site, and a fix reaches every site when it raises the design system's version (ADR 0054). In the core, a new look would need a release of the core; in the site template, every site would hold a copy that drifts.
- What a component may hold is content, which the intake must check before it is published, whoever draws it. The core already has one registry for the intake, the reader and the editor (ADR 0060), so declaring the design system's components there costs nothing new.
- The core cannot know a design's markup. Marks on the blocks and an event let it lead an editor to a block without knowing what a tab or an answer looks like.
- A design that reads what a component holds from its rendered HTML must parse a string. Hast carries the marks, which are properties of the rendered elements, and `Body` holds the rendering, not the source. Every component with a body is given it, since an Astro component cannot say what it wants before it is rendered, and reading a body's HTML costs little beside rendering it. No attribute may be called `content` already, so the prop hides none.
- A component that lays out its items by what it holds, cards, tabs, questions or a choice's answers split at their headings, could draw an item only from the tree, a component within as its plain rendering, an image without its lightbox, and a form or a post list not at all, while the default slot gave it the body whole or nothing. A slot for each block lets the core draw inside each item what only the core can. Named slots need no import of the core, which a design's package does not make (ADR 0054), and no reserved prop; Astro renders a slot only when a component asks for it, so a block left out costs nothing. The name follows the tree a component already reads for its layout, so the component needs no rule of its own to find a block's slot, and its prefix keeps it apart from `default` and from any slot the core may give later.
- A template that builds something beside the body from what it holds, such as a table of contents, reads its crossheads and the components that carry an anchor. The Markdown tree holds a component as a directive node and an anchor as raw HTML in a heading, and the HTML must be parsed; the tree `content` is holds each heading with the ID the page gives it and each block with its mark. A page's frontmatter, which builds what stands before the body, has no HTML of the body until it renders it, so the helper takes the entry and renders its `Content` itself, through Astro's container, the one way Astro gives to render a component outside the page's own rendering, as a feed renders a body. The page's own rendering, each component by the site's component and each post list by its presentation, is read from HTML a template has rendered, which a component around `Body` has from its slot.
- A wrapper with `display: contents` keeps its children's boxes, but not the selectors: a design that lays a component out by the child combinator or `:has(> …)`, as the design system's section does, would lose its layout in the preview alone, and a rule for a body's children, such as the space between them, would meet the wrapper rather than the component. A mark on the component's root changes no selector. An attribute's name is in camelCase, so a prop with hyphens hides none, and a component that places the mark chooses its root itself. The core looks at what a component rendered, since an Astro component cannot say beforehand what it renders, so a component that does not place the mark is marked as well.

## Considered and rejected

- **Rendering them in the core.** Every site would get the same components whether its design wants them or not, and every change of look would be a release of the core.
- **Rendering them in the site template** (`SITE-EXCEPTION.md`). Each site would carry its own copy from the day it was made, and a fix would reach one site at a time.
- **The body as mdast, the tree of its Markdown.** It holds no marks, which are the rendering's, and a design would draw the core's elements itself.
- **A tree only for a component that asks for it,** by a setting beside its path in `konzilo({ directives })`. One more setting for every site, to save a parse that costs little.
- **A component of the core's handed to a component as a prop,** under a reserved name, which it renders for a node of its tree. It takes a prop's name from every component, and a design's markup then holds the core's component, though it imports none.
- **A block's slot named by its mark,** the path `2.1` the live preview gives it. The public site has no marks, and they count a body's blocks as the field holds them, which the tree, read from the HTML, may count otherwise, a block of raw HTML say.
- **A template's tree read from the entry's stored HTML,** `entry.rendered.html`, with no rendering. Its images are placeholders that Astro fills in only as it renders the entry, so its tree would not be the one `content` is.
- **A template's tree of the page's own rendering from the entry,** `Body` rendered a second time through Astro's container. Every component of the body would be drawn twice, each dialog numbered apart from the page's, and the helper would need the page's address and page; a template that wants that rendering has it from `Body`'s HTML, which the helper reads.
- **A wrapper around every component in the preview.** Within another component's body it would stand between the two, inside the outer one's root, where that one's child selectors cannot see past it, so the page would lay out otherwise than on the public site.
- **A wrapper within another component too, for a component the core cannot mark.** It would stand inside the outer component's root all the same, for the few components that render no element of their own, whose blocks lead to them.
- **The mark placed by the page's script, from a comment before and after each component.** The page would stand unmarked until the script ran, and the HTML a design's tests read would hold comments rather than marks.

## Consequences

- The recommended reading is ranked by the core, which gives a template from none to three related posts by default; the design system draws them by its rule for a series of cards: one card keeps a column's width, two stand in two columns and three in three, and with none the template writes neither heading nor list.
- A design's small interface icons may stand inline in its stylesheet as SVG, since a page's policy allows `data:` images by default (ADR 0051); anything larger is a file. The design system's own marks are CSS shapes or inline `<svg>` in its markup, so a site that switches `data:` images off loses none of them.
- The design system has from the core what it needs of these contracts: a site takes the components' declarations and their check from its package (`componentsFrom` and `componentChecks`, ADR 0060), a component lays itself out by its body's tree in `content`, with the block marks on it, has the core draw each block it places through the block's own slot, and places its block's mark from `data-konzilo-block` on its own root, so that its layout's child selectors find in the live preview what they find on the public site.
- A component that passes the props it does not take on to its root leaves `content` out of them, `const { content, ...rest } = Astro.props`, since Astro writes an object it is given there as an attribute, `content="[object Object]"`, on the public site too, which the core cannot stop (`docs/site-design.md`). The rest holds the preview's mark, so its root holds that.
- The build's tests assert that each component with a body is given its tree beside its slot, a component within as its plain rendering, and none without a body; that in the live preview the tree's blocks hold their marks, so that a component drawn from it keeps them; that each component's mark stands on its root, placed by the component or by the core, with a wrapper only at the top of a body and none on the public site; and that a component that passes on the rest of its props, `content` left out, writes no `content` and holds its mark. They assert that a container that splits its body at its headings and draws each item's blocks through their slots has a component within drawn by the site's component, an image that opens in the lightbox, its dialog with a number of its own, and a post list drawn by its presentation, each with its mark in the live preview; that a block whose slot is not asked for is not rendered, while a list in it leaves its posts out of the lists below as it would drawn; and that a page whose components ask for no block's slot is rendered as it was before they could. They assert that a template's tree from its post's entry holds each crosshead with its ID and a component with an anchor as its plain rendering, is for a component's part the tree that component is given as `content`, holds no mark in a public build and the marks of its blocks in the live preview, by the build and by the dev server, and that the tree of the HTML a template rendered through `Body` holds a component as the site's component drew it, with its anchor, and a post list by its presentation, with the page's marks in the live preview. The live preview's acceptance tests assert, in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, that a design's child selectors lay out nested components as on the public site, and that a click on a nested component, on a block a component drew from its tree, or on a block drawn through its slot, and a field's request to show any of them, find that block, opening the `<details>` it stands in.
- What a component draws from its tree it draws itself: a component within stands there as its plain rendering, and an image without its lightbox, which the core adds to the slots alone. A design that wants them places the default slot, or a block's own slot for each block. A slot for a part smaller or larger than a block at the top of the body, a range of blocks or a block nested deeper, waits until a design asks for it. Where the core's scan of a body's HTML does not find at its top the nodes the tree has there, which the HTML the core renders does not give, since its reader writes the HTML of the tree it reads, but HTML a site gives Body of its own may, the component is given no block's slot rather than a block under another's name, and the build log says so, naming the post, or the page where there is none, and the component.
- The design system's package ships the declarations of these components as a JSON file and their check as a module, which a site names by their package specifiers (ADR 0060). Until its first release, a site either declares these components and draws them with styles of its own, or does without them.
