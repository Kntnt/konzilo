## Decision

- **Reserved paths.** Visitors' browsers reach a site's services at paths below `/_konzilo/`, on the site's own host and on its pre-launch address, behind its password there. Each service takes one path: `/_konzilo/search` goes to Typesense, and the rest to the service. No release page may be there; the checker and the build refuse one. The core's own files below the prefix are the exceptions: the page a form's post gets while the service does not answer (ADR 0045), and the consent scripts with their stylesheet below `/_konzilo/consent/`, which the web server serves as files, `GET` alone, before it passes the prefix to the service (ADR 0049).
- **One program in two roles,** the `visitors` and `editors` containers (ADR 0005), under limits, with SQLite (`node:sqlite`) in a volume of the customer's own, each listening on every address of its container, or on the one `KONZILO_LISTEN` names (ADR 0002). Only `editors` reaches the source repository, and only `editors` sends mail. It has no database server. `visitors` is in a network of its own that only the web server shares, with no way out. The service's data is one database, `service.sqlite`, in the volume `service-data`: the service's key, made at its first start, and each kind's storage. Both roles open it: `visitors` keeps what visitors send, and `editors` reads it for Admins and Editors, such as a form's submissions and the consent records. `visitors` also reads the releases the web server serves, read-only, for the consent revision of the release in use, and `editors` reads them too, read-only, for what the release in use answers, beside the web server's log of addresses not found, read-only as well (ADR 0040). Each change to what it holds is a step taken once, counted in the database's own `user_version`, so a later duty adds a step and an older database is brought up to it at the next start.
- **It accepts only kinds the core registers.** Each kind has a path, methods, content types, a size limit, a check and storage. A kind takes the requests to its path and every path below it. It may answer with JSON, `204` with nothing, `429` with `Retry-After`, or `303` back to a path on the site. The service refuses a method a kind does not take, a body over its limit and a body of a type it does not take, and answers `no-store` and `nosniff`. It never sets a cookie.
- **Limits:** each request within ten seconds and 64 MB; the web server cuts a body off at a megabyte, or a kind's own higher limit; over-limit bodies are refused by length, unread, and the connection closed once the client has sent the rest, which is thrown away, or two seconds after, so that a client still sending reads the refusal rather than a reset.
- **The web server passes on** the request's method, path, query, body with its type and length, the visitor's address, and `Origin`, `Referer` and `Sec-Fetch-Site`, and nothing else: no cookie, browser, password or language. It keeps no log of these requests. The address, and the host the request came to, go in headers of its own, `X-Konzilo-Client` and `X-Konzilo-Host`, which it sets whatever the visitor sent, as the dev server does locally. Every path below the prefix goes to `visitors` but `/_konzilo/search` and the consent scripts' files, `GET` and `HEAD`.
- **The service keeps them from every kind.** A kind gets only what it makes of them: whether the request came from a page of the site's own (`Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin`, or, from a browser that sends none, an `Origin` or else a `Referer` on the host the request came to or one of the site's public hosts); whether an address it holds is on one of those hosts; and a keyed digest of the address and a window of ten minutes, with the window's end. The key comes from a secret in the service's data. **No IP address, user agent or referrer is kept,** and no log of the requests is written. The digest is HMAC-SHA256 of the address and the window's number, under a key derived from the service's key; a window is one of the clock's own ten minutes, `:00` to `:10` and on, and what a kind counts by the digest it holds in memory alone, forgotten as the window ends.
- **For editors,** on the admin host, each call checked against Forgejo: the check before saving, the list of titles, status, the preview gate, share links and the pages in Sveltia, the forms' submissions below `/_konzilo/submissions` among them (ADR 0045), how the site mails them and how long it keeps them at `/_konzilo/mail`, a person's at `/_konzilo/personal-data`, the consent records at `/_konzilo/consent-records` ([ADR 0049](0049-manage-consent-with-konzilos-own-client-from-one-service-registry.md)), the counts of what the site's policy blocked at `/_konzilo/violations` ([ADR 0051](0051-write-security-headers-and-an-enforced-policy-into-each-release.md)), and the addresses visitors asked for in vain at `/_konzilo/not-found` (ADR 0040), each an Admin's alone, an Editor told only that (ADR 0003), the locks of the editor's forms at `/_konzilo/locks`, kept in the role's memory alone, not in the service's data ([ADR 0073](0073-lock-a-form-to-the-tab-that-has-it-open-and-refuse-a-save-older-than-the-latest.md)), a form's revisions at `/_konzilo/revisions` ([ADR 0074](0074-show-a-forms-revisions-from-the-source-history-compare-any-two-and-restore-one-into-the-form.md)), and the deleted posts, terms and image library entries at `/_konzilo/deleted` ([ADR 0077](0077-find-read-and-restore-a-deleted-post-term-or-image-library-entry-from-the-source-history.md)), both read from the source history and kept in the role's memory alone, and the incoming feeds at `/_konzilo/feeds`, an Admin's alone ([ADR 0079](0079-mirror-an-incoming-feeds-items-as-published-posts-that-only-the-feed-reader-changes.md)). Whoever may not write is told nothing there, not even which forms there are. The account that pushed each revision, and each deletion, the role reads from Forgejo's feed by the instance account's read-only token, `pushes-token`, which reaches beyond the source repository and can change nothing, and which it uses for the feed and a push's commits alone (ADR 0074).
- **Timed duties:** mail for each pending submission, within seconds, retention, at the start and each minute, each incoming feed at its interval, looked for each minute ([ADR 0079](0079-mirror-an-incoming-feeds-items-as-published-posts-that-only-the-feed-reader-changes.md)), spam after seven days, consent records after 13 months, the policy's violations 30 days after each was last seen, at the start and each minute, the web server's log of addresses not found folded into the counts, at the start and every five minutes (ADR 0040), expired share links, and unused previews.
- **What a build hands over.** Every public release holds its consent revision, with what its pages showed, beside its pages, and `visitors` keeps it as soon as the release is the one in use, before it decides on any record, so that visitors can send nothing from a new release that the service does not know. The service reads the forms from the site description of the code in use, again whenever its file changes, from the site's folder, mounted whole. A local copy works the same way.
- **Kept out of Git:** recipients lists, the mail adapter with its settings, retention, the incoming feeds with what is known of their items, and the service's secret, in SQLite, edited on a page: the recipients, the retention and the mail on the *Mail* page, and the feeds on the *Feeds* page, by an Admin alone.
- **What it forgets is gone.** A submission forgotten at its form's retention, or erased at a person's request, is overwritten where it stood, and the log of writes emptied, so that the database's free space, its log, and so a backup or an archive, hold nothing of it.
- **Its data joins** the archive, backups, restores and the drill (ADR 0006). A service down leaves the public pages answering; a form says it was not sent (ADR 0045).

## Why

- The page's own origin needs no CORS, and a Content Security Policy needs only `'self'`, and it works the same before and after launch and locally.
- A digest that changes every ten minutes counts one client and is gone with its window. It cannot be followed across windows, and no address is kept.
- Handing a kind the verdict alone keeps the address out of every kind's code, by construction.
- A store of its own lets backups, restores and archives carry what visitors send, and consent can be demonstrated.

## Considered and rejected

- **A services host per server.** It is cross-origin, so it needs CORS, policy entries and a certificate of its own.
- **The admin host.** It puts visitors on the login domain.
- **A beacon logged by the web server.** It loses request bodies.
- **No server-side records.** Consent could not be demonstrated.
- **A digest made by the web server,** or its rate limits. Caddy's standard build has no keyed hash among its placeholders and no keyed window.
- **A CSRF token.** No cookie or session authorizes anything here, so the origin is what matters.
