# ADR 0073: Lock a form to the tab that has it open, and refuse a save older than the latest

**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** Accepted. Settled by Thomas on 2026-11-23: every form in Sveltia is locked to the one tab that has it open, and any other Editor may take it over; a save from a form older than the latest save is refused, even where the two changed different fields; the editor keeps the lock, and the server's one guarantee is that an old copy cannot overwrite a newer one. The rest is a technical choice; Thomas may veto, among them, chosen in building it on 2026-11-23:

- **Where the locks live:** in the memory of the service's role for editors, at `/_konzilo/locks` on the admin host, not in its SQLite, since nothing of them outlasts the tabs that hold them. A restart forgets them, and each tab still open takes its own back at its next call, unless another tab has taken the form meanwhile, which that tab is then told as of a takeover.
- **How a tab says it is alive, and is told at once:** it asks for every lock and holds the call open until they change, for at most 25 seconds, then asks again; each call says it is alive, and a lock whose tab has not called for 90 seconds goes at the next sweep, every 10 seconds, so within two minutes. A form is held and taken over by `PUT /_konzilo/locks/<form>?tab=…`, and let go by `DELETE`, also as a page is left, by a call that outlives it.
- **A form and a tab by name:** a form is named as the editor's address names it, `<collection>/<entry>`; a tab names itself anew at each load, so that a tab copied with its session is another tab. A new entry has no lock until its first save gives it an address.
- **An Admin's forms** are the collections `settings` and `menus`; the service asks Forgejo whether the one asking is an Admin, as for the *Mail* page.
- **Until the service has answered,** a form opens read-only, saying it checks that no other tab has it open, and Sveltia looks for a draft backup of it only then. **Where the service does not answer,** within ten seconds, the editor lets the Editor work and save, since the check against the latest save still keeps an old copy from overwriting a newer one.
- **A form taken back:** a tab that takes back a form taken over from it keeps the unsaved changes it held as a draft backup, and shows them read-only from the latest save, saying they are from before the takeover, until the Editor opens the latest save, which lets the backup go; where it held none, the latest save opens to edit. Sveltia's own backup of a read-only form is made for this alone.
- **A lock ends** as its form is left, and as its page is closed, by a call that outlives the page, in every state in which the tab holds it, a draft backup shown read-only among them.
- **The read-only state** is Sveltia's own for a read-only collection, which draws its fields read-only, offers no Save and no Delete and keeps no backup, made to follow the lock by a patch. The core's own fields, which Sveltia does not tell, hold back in a read-only form every key, typing, pasting, dropping and button that would change something, and say `aria-readonly` to a screen reader, while what they hold stays to read, select and copy; a field group draws its fields read-only and offers no menu.
- **The latest save** is Sveltia's own check before a save, which asks Forgejo for the latest commit and compares the entry with the one the form was opened from; its warning and its dialog take the core's words, and *Save Anyway* is a Reload. The refusal names the later save by the account that pushed it and its moment, as the form's history has them ([ADR 0074](0074-show-a-forms-revisions-from-the-source-history-compare-any-two-and-restore-one-into-the-form.md)), never by the author a commit names; where Forgejo has no record of the account, it gives the moment alone, and where the history cannot be asked, neither. The reload keeps the Editor's changes as a draft backup, which the form then shows read-only, from the moment they were made, until the Editor opens the latest version, which lets the backup go. A backup is from before a later save where the entry it was made from is not the entry as it stands.
- **The retry:** a save Git turns away because another commit came first, as the branch having moved, which Sveltia tells from a 409 or 422 by reading the branch anew, or as a ref it could not lock, is tried again, at most three times more, a fifth of a second or more apart, each time after Sveltia has read the latest commit and found the entry itself unchanged; where it has changed, the save is refused as older than the latest. A save Forgejo answers with a server error may have gone in all the same: where the latest commit holds each file as the save wrote it, the save is done, and is never refused as older than itself; otherwise it is tried again in the same way. Any other answer, a forbidden save among them, is not tried again, and Sveltia shows its error.
- **A deletion from the list** holds each entry for the deletion alone, and lets it go after; an entry another tab holds is refused, naming who has it open.

## Decision

- **Every form is locked:** a post's, a term's, an image library entry's, the site settings', and each menu's in each language, each as one form. An **Editor** is anyone who writes, an Admin too; a Reviewer never enters Sveltia and is not touched.
- **A lock is held by a tab, not by a person.** Someone who opens a form they already have open in another tab, on this device or another, meets the same choice as anyone else, told that it is their own other tab.
- **Opening a locked form says who has it and since when,** and offers three ways on: back to the list, the form in its read-only state, or a **takeover**. The list marks each locked entry with the name of the Editor who has it.
- **The read-only state** is the form with every field inert, no Save and no Delete, and a line above it saying why. The same state serves three cases: reading a locked form, the form of a tab whose lock was taken over, and a draft backup that can no longer be saved.
- **A takeover tells the tab that had it at once,** by name and time. That tab's form turns read-only, with its unsaved changes still in its fields to read and copy, and never saves them.
- **Only someone who may save a form locks it.** An Editor who may not save it, such as one in the Editor role at the site settings or a menu, which are an Admin's ([ADR 0008](0008-give-customers-three-roles-admin-editor-and-reviewer.md)), takes no lock and is offered no takeover: the form opens read-only, saying it is for Admins, with the way back to the list.
- **A lock ends by itself** when its form is left or closed, and within two minutes after its tab stops answering. It never ends because its holder is idle: an Editor who needs the form takes it over.
- **The editor keeps the lock.** Before each save and each deletion, the form asks whether its tab still holds the lock, and refuses where it does not. A deletion is a change like any other and needs the lock, from the form or from the list.
- **The server's one guarantee is the latest save.** A save made from a form opened before a later save is refused, with a refusal naming the account that made the later save and when, even where the two changed different fields; nothing is merged. Sveltia's *Save Anyway* is gone. The same holds for whatever writes the source repository outside Sveltia, a developer's push or the instance administrator's: it takes no lock, and its commit makes every open form of what it changed stale.
- **A draft backup is never saved over a later save.** Sveltia offers a backup of unsaved changes as before only where the entry has not been saved since the backup was taken. Otherwise the backup is shown in the read-only state, to read and copy.
- **Two people saving different entries at once** both succeed: a save Git turns away because another commit came first is tried again, with the check against the latest save made anew.

## Why

- Two Editors in one form, each saving, overwrite each other, and neither knows until the work is gone. Sveltia warns, but its *Save Anyway* overwrites, and the lost version stays only in Git's history, where an Editor cannot reach it.
- A lock that can be taken over prevents that without ever leaving a form stuck behind someone who has gone home, so no timeout has to guess how long reading or thinking takes.
- A lock per tab, not per person, since two tabs of one person overwrite each other as two people do.
- Editors are trusted: everyone may publish ([ADR 0008](0008-give-customers-three-roles-admin-editor-and-reviewer.md)). The lock guards against mistakes, not against a person changing their own client, so the editor can keep it. The latest-save check covers what the lock cannot see, a tab back from a lost connection, and every write from outside Sveltia.
- Refusing always is predictable and easy to explain. Comparing the two saves, once an Editor can compare revisions, takes care of the rest; merging can come later if a site needs it.

## Considered and rejected

- **Merging changes to different fields.** Harder to explain, and a merge of two bodies is a guess.
- **A lock that ends after a time without activity.** It takes the form from someone reading or thinking.
- **Refusing a push of a locked entry on the server,** by a hook in Forgejo or in the intake. No hook exists yet, and the trust the roles already give makes it unnecessary.
- **Sending a reader of a locked post to its page in the live preview.** The preview does not show search, sharing or structured data, which are often what is read.
- **Dropping a draft backup that can no longer be saved.** Unsaved work is never lost, only never saved over someone else's.

## Consequences

- [ADR 0029](0029-configure-sveltia-from-the-description-and-patch-its-npm-build.md)'s patch of a retry when two people save at once is part of this decision, and *Save Anyway* is patched away.
- `GLOSSARY.md` defines the lock and the takeover, and a refusal covers a save older than the latest.
